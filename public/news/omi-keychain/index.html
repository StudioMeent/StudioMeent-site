“What a ridiculous assignment! Nobody owns a house, so why on earth would anyone need a keychain?”

Geen huis = geen sleutel

October 2026

We were asked to design a keychain for OMI. In a city with 100.000 woningzoekenden, we made one for a key most people don't have.

The insert is laser-engraved on clear plexiglass: an old house key, the number of people looking for a home in Rotterdam, and along the edge geen huis = geen sleutel.
